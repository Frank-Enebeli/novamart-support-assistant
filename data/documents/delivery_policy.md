# NovaMart Delivery Policy

NovaMart delivers orders throughout all 36 states in Nigeria and the Federal Capital Territory. Standard delivery is available nationwide.

Standard delivery costs NGN 2,500 per order. Orders with a merchandise value of NGN 100,000 or more qualify for free standard delivery within Nigeria. Additional charges may apply to locations classified as remote, and any additional fee will be shown before checkout is completed.

Standard delivery normally takes 2 to 5 business days after an order has been dispatched. Deliveries to remote locations may take 5 to 8 business days.

Express delivery is available in selected areas of Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt. Express delivery costs NGN 4,500 and normally takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. Express delivery availability is confirmed during checkout.

NovaMart currently ships internationally to Ghana, Kenya, the United Kingdom, Canada, and the United States. International delivery normally takes 7 to 14 business days after dispatch. International delivery charges are calculated at checkout based on the destination and package size.

Customers receiving international orders are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, or other charges imposed by their destination country.

Orders are not dispatched on Sundays or Nigerian public holidays.

Customers receive a dispatch confirmation email containing tracking information when their order leaves the warehouse. Tracking information may take several hours to become active.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed when delays are caused by customs processing, severe weather, transport disruptions, incorrect addresses, or other circumstances outside NovaMart's control.

If a delivery is significantly overdue, customers should contact NovaMart customer support with their order number. The AI support assistant cannot access live courier systems or determine the current location of an individual customer's parcel.
