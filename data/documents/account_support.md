# NovaMart Account and Password Support

Customers who forget their NovaMart password should use the "Forgot Password" option on the sign-in page.

NovaMart sends a password-reset link to the email address registered to the customer's account. Password-reset links expire 30 minutes after they are issued.

If a password-reset link has expired, the customer should request a new one rather than attempting to reuse the expired link.

For security, an account may be temporarily locked after 5 unsuccessful sign-in attempts. The temporary lock normally lasts 30 minutes.

Customers who continue to have difficulty signing in after the lock period may contact NovaMart customer support.

Customers who no longer have access to the email address registered to their NovaMart account must contact customer support. Additional identity verification may be required before account details can be changed.

Customers who believe another person has accessed their account should change their password immediately if they can still sign in and should contact NovaMart's security support channel.

NovaMart staff will never ask customers to provide their password or one-time verification code by email or chat.

The AI support assistant cannot see customer passwords, reset passwords, unlock accounts, change account email addresses, or access private account information.

Questions involving account ownership, suspected account takeover, private customer information, or security verification must be escalated to a human support representative.

Customers should not share passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information with the AI assistant.
