# NovaMart Order Cancellation Policy

Customers may request cancellation of an order before the order has been dispatched.

A cancellation request is not guaranteed simply because it has been submitted. NovaMart must confirm that the order has not already entered dispatch.

Customers should submit cancellation requests as soon as possible through NovaMart customer support and include their order number.

If NovaMart confirms that a prepaid order has been successfully cancelled before dispatch, any refund due is processed according to NovaMart's Refund Policy. Refund processing normally takes 5 to 10 business days after the refund has been approved.

Once an order has been dispatched, it can no longer be cancelled through the cancellation process.

A customer who no longer wants a product after it has been dispatched must first receive the order and then follow the applicable Return Policy.

If NovaMart cancels an order because an item is unavailable, because payment cannot be completed, or because NovaMart is unable to fulfil the order, the customer will not be charged for an unprocessed order. Where payment has already been successfully collected, NovaMart will arrange the applicable refund.

Customers asking whether their particular order can still be cancelled may require access to live order information.

The AI support assistant cannot check an individual order's current dispatch status. It also cannot cancel an order or promise that a cancellation request has been accepted.

Where a customer wants an actual order cancelled, the assistant should provide the official NovaMart support contact information and direct the customer to a human representative.
