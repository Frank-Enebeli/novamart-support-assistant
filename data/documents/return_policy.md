# NovaMart Return Policy

Most products purchased from NovaMart may be returned within 30 calendar days of the date the customer receives the order.

To qualify for a standard return, an item must be unused, in a resalable condition, and returned with its original packaging, accessories, manuals, and any promotional items supplied with it. Customers must provide the NovaMart order number or another acceptable proof of purchase.

Customers who simply change their mind are responsible for the cost of returning the product to NovaMart unless the product was delivered incorrectly or arrived damaged.

For hygiene and safety reasons, opened personal-care products, opened earphones, opened software products, and personalised products cannot normally be returned because of a change of mind.

A product that arrives damaged, defective, or different from what the customer ordered should be reported to NovaMart within 48 hours of delivery. Customers may be asked to provide photographs of the product, packaging, and shipping label.

Damaged or incorrectly supplied items do not need to be unused in order for the customer to report the problem.

Before sending a product back, customers should contact NovaMart support to obtain return instructions. Sending an item to the warehouse without the appropriate return information may delay processing.

Returned products are inspected after they arrive at NovaMart. Receiving a returned parcel does not automatically mean that the return has been approved.

Where a return is approved, any refund due is handled according to NovaMart's Refund Policy.

Warranty claims for products that develop faults after normal use should be handled under the Product Warranty Policy rather than the standard change-of-mind return process.
