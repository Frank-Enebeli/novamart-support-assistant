# NovaMart Product Warranty Policy

Selected products sold by NovaMart include a limited warranty against manufacturing defects.

Eligible electronic products have a 12-month warranty from the date of delivery unless the product listing states a different warranty period.

Eligible electronic accessories have a 6-month warranty from the date of delivery unless otherwise stated.

The warranty covers faults caused by defects in materials or workmanship during normal use.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, cracked screens caused by impact, liquid or water damage, misuse, improper installation, unauthorised repairs, normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage that does not affect operation, or damage caused by using an unsuitable power source.

Consumable items such as disposable batteries are not covered unless the product information specifically states otherwise.

A warranty may also be rejected where a product's serial number has been deliberately removed, altered, or made unreadable.

Customers making a warranty claim should provide their NovaMart order number, a description of the problem, and any photographs or video that reasonably demonstrate the fault.

NovaMart may require the product to be inspected before approving a warranty remedy. Initial warranty assessments normally take up to 5 business days after the required information or product has been received.

Depending on the fault and product, an approved warranty claim may result in repair, replacement, or another remedy permitted under NovaMart's warranty terms.

A customer who receives a product already damaged on arrival should use the damaged-product process described in the Return Policy rather than waiting to make a normal warranty claim.

The AI support assistant can explain warranty rules but cannot diagnose a product, approve a warranty claim, or confirm whether a specific customer's product is covered.
