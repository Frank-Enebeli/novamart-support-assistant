# NovaMart Payment Methods

NovaMart accepts several payment methods for online purchases.

Customers may pay using Visa, Mastercard, or Verve debit and credit cards that are accepted by NovaMart's payment processor.

Customers may also pay by bank transfer when the bank-transfer option is displayed during checkout. An order paid by bank transfer is not considered confirmed until NovaMart receives successful payment confirmation.

NovaMart does not offer cash on delivery.

A customer's order may remain in a pending state while payment is being verified. Customers should not make a second payment simply because confirmation is delayed unless NovaMart support specifically instructs them to do so.

If a customer believes they were charged more than once for the same order, they should contact NovaMart support with the order number and evidence of the transactions. Duplicate-charge complaints may require investigation by the payment team.

NovaMart employees and the AI support assistant will never ask a customer to provide a full card number, card PIN, online-banking password, or one-time password through chat.

Customers should immediately stop a conversation and contact NovaMart through its official support channels if someone claiming to represent NovaMart asks for sensitive banking credentials.

For payment disputes, unknown charges, suspected payment fraud, or transactions that require access to private customer records, the AI support assistant must refer the customer to a human support representative.

The AI assistant can explain NovaMart's published payment policies, but it cannot verify whether a particular payment succeeded, reverse a charge, confirm a bank transfer, or access a customer's payment account.
