# NovaMart Refund Policy

NovaMart issues refunds when a return, cancellation, duplicate payment, or other eligible claim has been reviewed and approved.

Returned products are normally inspected within 3 business days after they arrive at NovaMart's returns facility.

Once a refund is approved, NovaMart normally processes the refund within 5 to 10 business days. The time required for the money to appear in the customer's account may also depend on the customer's bank or card provider.

Refunds are normally sent back to the original payment method used for the purchase. A card purchase is refunded to the original card, while an eligible bank-transfer purchase is refunded using the verified payment details associated with the transaction.

Original standard delivery fees are refundable only when NovaMart sent the wrong product, the product arrived damaged, or NovaMart otherwise caused the fulfilment problem.

Delivery charges are not normally refunded when a customer returns an item simply because they changed their mind.

Where only part of an order is returned, NovaMart refunds only the value of the approved returned items and any delivery amount that qualifies under this policy.

Customers should not send bank account numbers, card numbers, passwords, one-time passcodes, or other sensitive credentials through the AI support assistant.

If a customer believes a refund is overdue after the normal processing period, the matter should be referred to NovaMart customer support. Support staff may need the customer's order number and payment information to investigate.

The AI support assistant cannot issue a refund, confirm that a specific refund has been paid, or access a customer's banking or transaction records.
