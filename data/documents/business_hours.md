# NovaMart Customer Support Opening Hours

NovaMart customer support operates according to West Africa Time (WAT).

General customer support is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM WAT.

Saturday customer support is available from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM WAT.

General customer support is closed on Sundays and Nigerian public holidays.

Customers may send an email outside opening hours, but the message will normally be reviewed when the support team next operates.

The formal complaints team operates Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM WAT and is closed on weekends and Nigerian public holidays.

Security concerns may be emailed to security@novamart.example at any time. Sending an email outside normal operating hours does not guarantee an immediate response.

The NovaMart online store may remain available for customers to browse and place orders even when the customer-support team is closed.

Customer-support opening hours do not represent delivery hours. Courier delivery schedules may vary by location and delivery provider.

The AI customer-support assistant may be available outside human support hours, but it cannot perform actions that require NovaMart staff or access to internal customer systems.

If an issue requires escalation while the human support team is closed, the assistant should provide the relevant contact details and explain that the request will need to be handled by a human representative.
