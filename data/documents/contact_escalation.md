# NovaMart Contact and Complaint Escalation

Customers can contact NovaMart customer support by email at support@novamart.example.

Customers may also contact the fictional NovaMart support line at +234 700 000 6627 during customer-support opening hours.

General questions about published NovaMart policies can be handled by the AI support assistant when the necessary information exists in the knowledge base.

A customer should be referred to a human representative when the request requires access to a specific customer's order, payment, account, or other private records.

Human escalation is also required for payment disputes, suspected fraud, account-security concerns, formal complaints, repeated unresolved delivery problems, requests to speak with a person, and cases where the knowledge base does not contain enough information to answer safely.

Formal complaints may be sent to complaints@novamart.example. Customers should include their order number where relevant and a clear description of the problem.

NovaMart aims to acknowledge formal complaints within 2 business days. More complex investigations may take longer.

Suspected account compromise or security incidents should be reported to security@novamart.example.

Customers should never send passwords, full payment-card numbers, card PINs, or one-time passwords through email or chat.

The AI support assistant has no access to NovaMart's private customer systems.

It cannot access an individual account, track a specific customer's live order, verify a payment, issue a refund, cancel an order, change a password, or confirm that NovaMart employees have completed any of those actions.

When a customer asks the assistant to perform one of those actions, it should explain its limitation and provide the appropriate human support contact information instead of pretending the action was completed.
